Who We Are

Our Story & Values

A mission born from a deep sense of social responsibility and love, working tirelessly to uplift underprivileged families across Haryana.

Empowering Lives

Carrying a Noble Ideology Forward

KCG Foundation is a registered, public-benefit non-profit organization established in loving memory of Late Sh. Kailash Chand Gupta. Guided by his vision of selfless service, the foundation targets the core issues holding back families in underprivileged pockets of Haryana.

We believe that social change is a collective endeavor. Our focus on educational scholarships, mid-day nutrition drives, and community health camps addresses the immediate physical and intellectual needs of families, enabling children to build a secure, self-reliant future.

Historical Legacy

The Journey of Inspiration

Trace the milestones of Late Sh. Kailash Chand Gupta's humanitarian impact and the growth of the foundation established in his honor.

1953

A Humble Beginning

Late Sh. Kailash Chand Gupta was born in Haryana on 23rd September. From an early age, he was deeply influenced by the rural community around him and developed a strong value system focused on integrity and charity.

1978

Empowerment at the Grassroots

Recognizing the struggles of daily-wage earners and construction workers in local industries, he initiated informal support circles to help their families access basic medical care and resolve housing issues.

1996

The Push for Child Literacy

Firmly believing that education is the ultimate equalizer, he started sponsoring the primary schooling fees and book supplies of local children out of his personal savings, ensuring no child drops out due to costs.

2015

A Legacy Entrusted

Sh. Kailash Chand Gupta left for his heavenly abode on 23rd April. His family and community circles resolved to institutionalize his life's philanthropic actions, ensuring his mission to serve humanity continues.

2016

KCG Foundation is Born

The foundation was formally registered as a non-profit. The team launched structured scholarship programs, established regular mid-day meal nutrition cycles, and expanded welfare drives to touch thousands of lives in Haryana.
