Our Core Mission

A Lifelong Mission of Love & Legacy

KCG Foundation is a non-profit, public benefit organization rooted in the philosophy of Late Sh. Kailash Chand Gupta. Founded in his memory, the Foundation works dedicatedly in the rural and semi-urban pockets of Haryana to empower unprivileged segments of society.

Our focus lies heavily on basic childhood education, comprehensive family welfare, nutritional security, and extending medical aid to underprivileged workers and their households.

Honoring Our Inspiration

Late Sh. Kailash Chand Gupta

23 September 1953 — 23 April 2015

A gentleman of silent compassion and tremendous humanitarian ideals, Sh. Kailash Chand Gupta believed that community growth is only authentic when it benefits the masses. He dedicated his life to facilitating support channels for low-income workers and educating local children.

His core values remain our guiding light. Every initiative undertaken by KCG Foundation is designed to carry his quiet, powerful legacy forward to the children of Haryana.

Scholarship & School Support
