Programs that Change Lives

Direct Action Initiatives

Our Operational Focus Areas

We deploy resources directly at the grassroots level, ensuring every donation makes a tangible, measurable difference in Haryana's communities.

Education

Scholarship & School Support

For children of daily-wage and agricultural laborers, the cost of uniforms, notebooks, school bags, and primary tuition is often prohibitive. We sponsor fees, distribute books, and supply uniforms to ensure that finances never force a child to drop out of school.

500+ Children Enrolled
12+ Rural Schools Assisted
Sponsor Child Volunteer
Nutrition

Mid-Day Meal & Food Security

A hungry child cannot focus, learn, or grow. KCG Foundation runs regular nutritional drives and organizes structured mid-day food sessions in community education spaces, providing healthy, wholesome meals to underprivileged children in need.

10k+ Meals Distributed
Weekly Nutritional Camps
Sponsor Meals Volunteer
Welfare

Worker & Family Welfare

Our commitment extends beyond children to the daily-wage laborers who form the backbone of the region. We host periodic free health check-ups, distribute essential hygiene kits, and assist labor families in accessing government welfare and livelihood schemes.
