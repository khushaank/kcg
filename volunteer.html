Join Our Cause

Give back to society. Settle directly at the grassroots to teach primary school children, organize mid-day nutritional meals, or support health campaigns.

Why Join KCG?

Rewards of Selfless Service

Volunteering is not just about helping others; it is about self-growth, community bonding, and creating lasting ripples of hope.

Direct Grassroots Impact

See the direct result of your tutoring or food serving drives within hours. Bring instantaneous smiles to little faces.

Skill & Career Growth

Acquire leadership, team coordination, project management, and public speaking skills. We provide official certificates upon completion.

Inspiring Community

Meet, collaborate, and form lifelong connections with like-minded changemakers, volunteer doctors, and local trustees.

Active Openings

Volunteer Support Roles

Select the operational role that aligns best with your professional expertise or personal hobbies. We accommodate both short-term weekend and full-time long-term support plans.

Primary Education Tutor

Teach elementary English, basic arithmetic, or creative painting/sketching at our semi-urban centers. Help local children build solid reading habits.

Food Security Assistant

Assist our food logistics team during weekly distribution camps. Coordinate hot meals setup and ensure clean nutritional delivery to children.

Health Camp Facilitator

Highly suited for medical students, certified practitioners, or nurses. Assist senior doctors in setting up checkup clinics and logging patient symptoms.
